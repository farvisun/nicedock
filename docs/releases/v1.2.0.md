NiceDock can now stand on the left or right edge of the screen as a vertical column.

**New**
- **Vertical Dock**: pick Left or Right (top, center or bottom) under Settings › Layout › Fixed. Widgets keep their
  look and stack top to bottom; separators, spaces, running apps and running dots follow the column.
- **Reveal from the Side**: a new layout that keeps NiceDock hidden until the pointer touches the left or right edge.
- **Replace the macOS Dock on a side**: Replace the macOS Dock and Replace & Auto-Hide get a Screen Edge setting
  (Bottom, Left, Right).
- **Vertical floating Dock**: Floating gets an Orientation setting.
- Widget panels open beside a column, ⌘-drag reorders along it, profile tabs list beside it, and Reserve Dock Space
  and the Window Manager widget keep windows clear of it. Settings warns when the macOS Dock sits on the same edge.
- The AI Assistant can set the new placements (`set_layout`: `reveal_edge`, `replace_edge`, `floating_vertical`).

**Install**
1. Download `NiceDock.dmg` and drag NiceDock into Applications, or `brew install --cask farvisun/tap/nicedock`.
2. NiceDock is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple, so macOS stops the first launch. Open
   System Settings › Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**, or run
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NiceDock.app` once.

Already on 1.1.0 or later: NiceDock offers this update itself (Check for Updates… in the menu bar menu).
